Senior Machine Learning Engineer

Company: NextGen AI Solutions
Location: Remote / Hybrid

About the Role:
We are seeking an experienced Senior Machine Learning Engineer to design, build, and deploy state-of-the-art NLP and predictive AI solutions. You will work on cutting-edge deep learning models, transformer architectures, and scalable data pipelines.

Requirements & Must-Haves:
- Strong proficiency in Python and SQL.
- Deep expertise in Machine Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Deep Learning.
- Hands-on experience with PyTorch, Scikit-learn, and Transformers (Hugging Face / spaCy).
- Experience containerizing and deploying models using Docker and Kubernetes.
- Familiarity with Cloud platforms (AWS or Azure).
- Minimum 3+ years of professional experience in machine learning or AI engineering.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related engineering discipline.

Preferred & Good to Have:
- Experience with FastAPI, Microservices, and REST API development.
- Knowledge of MLOps, CI/CD pipelines, and Git.
- Experience with Large Language Models (LLMs) and vector databases.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and implement scalable machine learning models and NLP pipelines.
- Collaborate with software engineers to integrate AI models into production microservices.
- Optimize inference latency, monitor model performance, and maintain automated retraining workflows.
